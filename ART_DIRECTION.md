# Art Direction — Crew of One V2

Original IP. Nothing here references or copies any film franchise. "Giant
mech vs. sea-titans in a storm-lit port city" is the genre; every name,
silhouette, mark and sound is ours.

## Fiction (one paragraph)

The **AEGIS WATCH** program fields multi-crew war machines — one mech, one
crew, every station a person. The enemy are **Abyssals**: bio-titans that
walk out of the trench into the coastal district of **Port Meridian**.
Runs are holding actions: the crew customizes the machine in Hangar Bay 07,
deploys into the district, and holds as the threat clock climbs.

## Visual pillars

1. **Weight** — the mech is architecture that walks. Small head, wide
   chest, layered plates, visible actuation. The camera sits close and low;
   scale cues (cars, streetlights, doors, deck crew) do the measuring.
2. **A lived-in district, not a level** — zoned architecture (glass core,
   concrete commercial, brick residential, corrugated industrial quarter,
   waterfront cranes), road markings, parked traffic, rooftop clutter,
   entrance awnings, billboards. Windows glow independent of wall tint.
3. **Weather is the stage light** — a rolling front system (storm, rain,
   overcast, clear night, golden hour, dawn) drives sky, fog, key/rim/fill,
   cloud deck, rain density and lightning. Rain is fine wind-sheared
   streaks, never screen-filling squares.
4. **Restraint** — filmic tonemapping, bloom only on genuine emitters,
   one accent per material family. Black is never crushed (ambient floor +
   camera fill keep the hull readable in every front).

## Palette

- Environment: deep blue-black glass (#05070c–#33344f range), sodium amber
  practicals (#ffca7a), emergency red (#ff4d5e), neon accents used sparingly.
- Mech paint: curated presets (Harbor Watch, Nightline, Aurora, Vermilion
  Guard, Pale Sentinel, Onyx Protocol). Custom colours are luminance-clamped
  server-side so the machine stays readable against the night district.
- Abyssals: desaturated organic hides with bioluminescent vent lines —
  the glow marks the weak seams and telegraphs.

## Lighting rig (in order of authority)

key (weather sun/moon, shadowed) → rim (cool, back-left) → fill (warm,
practical side) → camera fill (soft, follows the boom) → hemisphere +
ambient floor → practicals (lamps, signals, windows, signs, fires).

## Type & UI

Command-interface: thin steel borders on blue-black glass, uppercase
letter-spaced labels, mono numerals, cyan telemetry / amber caution / red
alert. No rounded arcade buttons, no drop shadows, no yellow prototype
banners.
