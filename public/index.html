cinematic co-op mech combat where every pilot controls a critical system

HOW TO PLAY (30 seconds)

Your crew pilots ONE giant mech. Each pilot is a different body part. The danger clock never stops climbing — kill for credits, spend them at glowing SUPPLY BEACONS, and survive. Press Q to ping targets for your crew.

01 / THE LEGS
W A S D walk   SPACE kick
The kick is mighty, but you're on one leg. Falling over is your fault.
02 / THE ARMS
MOUSE aim   CLICK punch (L/R buttons = L/R fist)
Punches wind up before they land. Start swinging BEFORE the crab arrives.
03 / THE HEAD
MOUSE aim   HOLD CLICK charge eye laser
3 seconds to charge; the mech slows to a crawl while firing. Sweep the beam.

Named kaiju arrive as the danger clock climbs. Fuel tanks explode when hit (use them), repair stations heal you (monsters wreck them), caches hold credits. Roles shuffle every run. The mech turns to face wherever the HEAD looks.
