CREWOFONE

2–8 pilots. One war machine. Every station is a person.

FIELD MANUAL (30 seconds)

Your crew pilots ONE giant mech. Each pilot runs a different station. In the hangar you build the machine together; in the field you keep it standing. Press Q to mark targets for your crew.

LOCOMOTION — THE LEGS
W A S D walk   SPACE kick
The kick is devastating, but you're on one leg. Balance is your responsibility.
STRIKE — THE ARMS
MOUSE aim   CLICK punch (L/R = L/R fist)
Punches wind up before they land. Commit early, follow through.
FIRE CONTROL — THE HEAD
MOUSE aim   HOLD CLICK charge the beam
Three seconds to charge; the mech slows while firing. Sweep the beam across the pack.

Named Abyssals surface as the danger clock climbs. Fuel tanks detonate when struck. Repair pads rebuild plate. Stations rotate every run. The hull turns to face wherever FIRE CONTROL looks.
